LockedIn is a single-purpose tool: **LeetCode email automation**. You set up a
study roadmap once, and it nags you daily via email until you finish it.

## How it works, in one paragraph

You sign in, create a roadmap (a named, time-boxed plan — a start date, an end
date, and a daily send time), and upload the list of problems that make it up.
From then on, LockedIn sends a daily email with the next problems on your
list, your progress so far, and a quote. If you fall behind, tomorrow's email
carries more problems to keep you on pace to finish by your end date — and if
your end date passes with problems still open, it keeps nagging rather than
going quiet.

## What it deliberately doesn't do (yet)

No AI-generated roadmaps, no gamification (XP, levels), no notification
channels beyond email, and no analytics. These are real directions being kept
open — the data model is shaped so they don't require a rewrite later — but
none of them ship today. See [Features](/docs/product/features) for what does.
