LockedIn is a single-purpose tool: **LeetCode email automation**. You set up a
study roadmap once, and it nags you daily via email until you finish it.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step>
    ### Sign in

    Roadmaps belong to an account, so this comes first.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Create a roadmap

    A named, time-boxed plan: a start date, an end date, and the time of day
    you want to be emailed.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Upload the problems

    The list of LeetCode problems the roadmap works through — each with a
    title, a link, and a difficulty.
  </Step>

  <Step>
    ### Get nagged

    From then on, one email per day per roadmap: the next problems on your
    list, your progress so far, and a quote. Fall behind and tomorrow's email
    carries more, to keep you on pace for your end date.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout title="Your end date is a goal, not a wall">
  If it passes with problems still open, LockedIn keeps nagging rather than
  going quiet. [Archiving](/docs/product/features) is how a roadmap stops.
</Callout>

## What it deliberately doesn't do (yet)

No AI-generated roadmaps, no gamification (XP, levels), no notification
channels beyond email, and no analytics.

These are real directions being kept open — the data model is shaped so they
don't require a rewrite later — but none of them ship today. See
[Features](/docs/product/features) for what does, and the
[roadmap](/docs/engineering/roadmap) for which version each one lands in.
