## Sign in

Authenticate to access your roadmap(s).

## Create a roadmap

A roadmap is a named, time-boxed plan: a name, a start date, an end date, and
a daily email send time.

## Upload questions

Upload or curate the list of LeetCode problems that make up the roadmap. Each
problem has a title, a link, and a difficulty.

## View progress

See a roadmap's progress in-app: problems completed out of the total, and
days elapsed out of the total.

## Mark complete

Mark a problem as solved. This is one-directional — un-marking a problem
isn't supported yet. Progress is recorded as an append-only history, so this
is a deliberate scope choice, not a technical limitation: adding "un-mark"
later doesn't require losing that history.

## Archive a roadmap

Archive a roadmap to stop it nagging you, and unarchive it later. An archived
roadmap keeps all its data — archiving doesn't delete anything.

## The daily email

The centerpiece — see [The daily email](/docs/product/daily-email) for how
many problems you get and why that number changes.
