---
title: The daily email
description: How many problems you get each day, and why that number changes.
---

Each day, per roadmap, you get one email containing:

- **One to five problems**, each with its title, a link, and its difficulty
- Your progress — problems completed out of total, days elapsed out of total
- A quote

## Why the count changes

The number of problems isn't fixed at roadmap creation — it's recomputed
every day from how many problems remain and how many days are left:

```
problemsToday = min(5, max(1, ceil(remaining / daysLeft)))
```

If you keep pace, that's usually 1. If you fall behind, the count rises
gradually to spread the catch-up over your remaining days, rather than
dumping a punishing backlog on you all at once — capped at 5, because a
twenty-problem email is noise, not a nudge.

## Past your end date

Your end date is a goal, not a wall. If it passes with problems still open,
LockedIn keeps nagging — the daily count stays capped at 5 — until you either
finish every problem or archive the roadmap.
