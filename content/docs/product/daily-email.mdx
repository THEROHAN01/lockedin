times before your send time comes round.

## Why the count changes

The number of problems isn't fixed when you create the roadmap. It is
recomputed on every send, from how many problems remain and how many days are
left:

```text
problemsToday = min(5, max(1, ceil(remaining / daysLeft)))
```

If you keep pace, that's usually 1. If you fall behind, the count rises
gradually to spread the catch-up over your remaining days, rather than dumping
a punishing backlog on you all at once.

<Callout title="Why it stops at five">
  A twenty-problem email is noise, not a nudge — past a handful you stop reading
  it, which defeats the entire point of the product.
</Callout>

Because the count is derived rather than stored, editing a roadmap's dates or
its problem list changes tomorrow's email immediately. There is nothing
cached to invalidate.

## Past your end date

Your end date is a goal, not a wall. If it passes with problems still open,
LockedIn keeps nagging — still capped at five a day — until you either finish
every problem or [archive the roadmap](/docs/product/features).
